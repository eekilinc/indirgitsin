İndir Gitsin — Privacy policy
Updated: 31 August 2026

Developer/project account: eekilinc
Source: https://github.com/eekilinc/indirgitsin

On-device data
The app does not create user accounts. Links, titles, download history and settings are stored on your device. The latest 50 history records are displayed; records may remain until you clear history or app data. Clear history from Library/Settings. History and settings are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer by the app's backup rules.

Files
Completed files are in the shared Download folder and can be accessed by other apps with permission. Uninstalling the app may not remove them; delete them from Downloads or a file manager.
Partial transfers stay in private, non-backed-up app storage. Successful transfers are cleaned up. Inactive parts older than seven days are removed on a subsequent app launch. Settings also lets you delete inactive partial downloads.

Network services
YouTube/Google servers receive requests to resolve links, transfer media and load thumbnails. They can receive your IP address, requested video/playlist identifier and request/device information. The cipher component may use a WebView and YouTube verification requests, which can create local web data. Google's own policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
GitHub API is used for release checks; GitHub files in ZemerTeam/zemer-cipher provide cipher configuration. GitHub receives IP addresses and request information: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
The app does not send video identifiers to public Cobalt/Piped/Invidious instances. No developer-operated analytics or advertising SDK has been added. This does not mean that network providers process no data.

Permissions
Internet/network state support transfers; foreground services support long downloads; notification permission supports progress notifications. Android 7–9 requires storage permission for the shared download directory. Android 10+ uses MediaStore. Camera, microphone, contacts and location permissions are not requested.

Control and contact
Clear cache in Settings, or clear private app data in Android settings. Clipboard suggestions are processed locally; choosing to resolve a link sends a request to its service.
General privacy questions: https://github.com/eekilinc/indirgitsin/issues
This is public: do not post personal data, passwords or private links. The developer cannot access third-party server data; direct requests about those services to their own privacy channels.
